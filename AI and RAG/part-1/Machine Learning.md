Machine Learning is a subset of AI where models learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions. Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses multi-layer neural networks to learn complex patterns, especially from unstructured data like images, audio, and text. Generative AI focuses on generating new content such as text, code, images, or audio, and modern generative AI systems are largely powered by deep learning models such as Transformers. LLMs are a major example of Generative AI.
